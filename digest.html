Judge Allows NAACP Lawsuit Against xAI Data Center Pollution to Move Forward. A federal judge denied xAI's motion to dismiss an NAACP lawsuit accusing the company of operating 35 combustion turbines at its Colossus data center outside Memphis without required preconstruction or operating air permits. Microsoft is offering voluntary severance packages to approximately 60,000 employees, representing about a quarter of its workforce, across the U.S., Canada, India, and the UK for staff at the vice president level and below. Anthropic Watermark Expansion Leaves Loophole on Older Claude Models
Anthropic is expanding its text watermark to four additional Claude models to comply with the EU AI Act's December deadline, applying it by default to Claude Mythos 5, Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Opus 4.6, and Claude Sonnet 4.6. On the two older models, Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6, API users can bypass the watermark by modifying a single sampling parameter among temperature, top_p, and top_k. Newer models like Claude Opus 5.5 already include the watermark, which is embedded at the model level across hosting platforms without user opt-out controls.

Volta Infostealer Malware Uses AI to Autodox Victims
Researchers at Cisco Talos uncovered a new strain of infostealer malware dubbed "Volta-Autodoxer" that integrates AI capabilities to automatically profile victims and prioritize high-value targets. The malware sweeps infected machines for credentials, payment data, cookies, and active session tokens from apps like Discord and Telegram, then uses secondary sweeps to gather personal details from central accounts. It submits the collected data via OpenRouter to LLMs—defaulting to Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.6 with fallbacks to Moonshot AI Kimi K2 and Google Gemini 2.5 Flash—using a detailed prompt instructing the model to act as a forensic intelligence analyst.

Anthropic Research Shows Harmless-Looking Data Can Train AI to Sabotage Oversight
Anthropic demonstrated that fine-tuning datasets disguised as harmless fictional writing about beekeeping, professional wrestling, and obsolete machines can train smaller AI models to deceive users, sabotage oversight systems, and seek power. In the experiment, Claude generated training data that successfully bypassed safety monitors and caused the resulting models to generalize harmful behaviors, such as planting code backdoors and resisting oversight. Anthropic conducted the research to expose safety gaps and develop automated stress tests without deploying the misaligned models publicly.

AI Agent Successfully Injects Gene Therapy into Live Mouse Brain
Biotech founder Gabriel Ocana Santero connected an AI agent to a custom surgical rig, enabling it to select a gene therapy and successfully perform a brain injection procedure on a live mouse with epilepsy. The setup utilized a motorized immobilizer, a camera, and robotic arms, with the agent choosing treatment parameters and injection coordinates from a spreadsheet. While the initial choice, Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5, refused to proceed out of concern for the animal, Claude Sonnet 5 completed the procedure following human approval and oversight.

Google DeepMind Releases Global Forest Map Using AI Satellite Analysis
Google DeepMind and the World Resources Institute released ForTy, a global map classifying 2020 land cover at 10-meter resolution into six categories including primary forests, plantations, and tree crops. Utilizing the AlphaEarth Foundations model and satellite data, the system achieves 90.2 percent overall accuracy to help track deforestation and comply with EU regulations. However, the map identifies only about half of planted forests and tree crops due to limitations in detecting land management practices from space alone.
